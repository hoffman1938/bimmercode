2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security
  • Display forum posts and enable community features
  • Investigate abuse, spam, and violations of our Terms

We do not use your data for targeted advertising or third-party marketing.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our site:

  • Essential Cookies — For logging in (session tokens) and storing your language and saved codes.
  • Analytics — We use Google Analytics and Yandex Metrika to understand how visitors interact with our website. These tools use cookies to collect anonymous usage data.

Advertising Partners (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. To provide relevant ads, Google and its partners use advertising cookies.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data may only be shared in limited circumstances:

  • Legal compliance — if required by law or court order
  • Infrastructure — Cloudflare processes requests as part of delivering the Service
  • Public forum posts — content you post is publicly visible to other users

5. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text)
  • All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Session tokens are cryptographically signed and expire automatically
  • Database access is restricted to server-side code only
No method of internet transmission is 100% secure. Use a strong, unique password.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Contact us to ask what data we hold about your account
  • Correction — Update your username and email from your Profile page at any time
  • Account Deletion — Request account deactivation by contacting us. Public forum posts may remain visible
  • Data Export — No automated export tool exists; you may copy your posts manually

To make a request, contact us at codesbimmer@gmail.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain account data (email, username, password hash) for as long as your account exists. Forum posts are retained indefinitely as they are part of the public community knowledge base.

If you request account deactivation, your profile will be removed and you will no longer be able to log in. Forum posts may remain visible under your username as part of existing threads.

8. Age Restriction

BimmerCodes is intended for users who are at least 16 years of age. By registering, you confirm that you meet this age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us and we will remove the account.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at codesbimmer@gmail.com or via our Contact Page.
