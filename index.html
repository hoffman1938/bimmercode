BMW Diagnostic Trouble Codes Database

BimmerCodes.net is a comprehensive BMW DTC database that helps you diagnose and fix issues with your BMW vehicle. Our free tool supports all BMW models including E-series (E30, E36, E46, E60, E90), F-series (F10, F30, F80), and G-series (G20, G30, G80).

Supported BMW Diagnostic Systems

Our database includes codes for all major BMW systems: DME/DDE (engine management), EGS (automatic transmission), ABS/DSC (stability control), SRS (airbag systems), KOMBI (instrument cluster), CAS (car access system), FRM (footwell module). Compatible with INPA, ISTA/D, ISTA/P, Rheingold, Carly, BimmerCode, and ELM327 adapters.
